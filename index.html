Safety & progression

With prior hip labrum surgery and restricted internal rotation, keep all movements within a comfortable range. Stop sharp pinching, catching, new back or radiating pain. Have a qualified PT or coach check hip hinge and mechanics before kettlebell swings, loaded rotational power, maximal golf-speed work or jumping. Advanced drills in the library are not automatically scheduled.

Weeks 1–4: 2 sets per main lift in week 1, progressing toward 3 as tolerated; 3–4 reps in reserve. Weeks 5–8: gradually increase load or reps while keeping form. Weeks 9–12: optional low-impact circuits and carefully selected golf sequencing drills; no obligation to add jumps or swings. Your equipment: 18 lb KB and 5/10/15/25/40 lb dumbbells. Suggested weights are editable.

Your physical therapist's handout recommends its Day 1 and Day 2 routines 1–2 times daily. The app integrates selected movements into training days, but does not replace that clinician's prescribed frequency. Follow their instructions if you are actively being treated.
